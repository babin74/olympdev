Alice hands Bob two integers written on a scrap of paper and asks him to add
them together before the bus arrives. Bob is fast at arithmetic, but even he
would rather have a program do it.

Given two integers $a$ and $b$, print their sum $a + b$.

\InputFile

The input contains two integers $a$ and $b$
($-10^9 \le a, b \le 10^9$), separated by a space.

\OutputFile

Print one integer: the sum $a + b$.
